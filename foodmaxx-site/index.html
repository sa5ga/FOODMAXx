Fridge

How the kitchen works
  1. Tap + to open the fridge. Tap a food to drop it in, or drag it anywhere.
  2. Swipe across a food to slice a smaller portion.
  3. Hold one food on another to mix a meal. Hold a slice on any food to stick it on, cut side first.
  4. Tap a whole food for a bigger serving (up to 6). Space works too. Slices stay one size.
  5. Right-click, or press and hold, to see FODMAPs and nutrition.
  6. Throw it into the prep box to save the meal.
